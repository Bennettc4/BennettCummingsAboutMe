About

Why marketing, and why album rollouts

I'm a Marketing student at Tulane's Freeman School of Business, minoring in AI Literacy and Digital Media & Marketing Communications. I care about the moments where a brand — or an artist — decides how the world meets their next chapter. Rollouts like Charli XCX's Brat, built by creative directors like Terrence O'Connor and Imogene Strauss, are what pulled me toward this path: a single color and a lowercase font turned into a cultural moment.

Outside of marketing, I'm into film photography and developing my own rolls, video editing, and house music — all things that show up in how I think about visual storytelling.
